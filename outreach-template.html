Outreach Directory Template

Key contacts and institutions for sourcing doughnut data. Customize for your city.

How to use this template

For each data gap in your doughnut portrait, identify the relevant local institution. Typical sources by dimension:

  • Food: Local food bank, county health dept, USDA regional office
  • Health: County health services, local hospitals, state health dept
  • Education: School district, state dept of education, local university
  • Income & Work: Census Bureau (ACS), BLS regional office, workforce development board
  • Housing: HUD regional office, local housing authority, continuum of care
  • Water: City/county water department, state water board
  • Energy: Local utility, state energy commission, community energy programs
  • Climate/Air: City sustainability office, EPA regional office, state air board
  • Biodiversity: State fish & wildlife, local land trust, regional conservation district
  • Social Equity: Community foundation, equity office, National Equity Atlas
  • Political Voice: County elections office, secretary of state

Example: Community Data Hub

Cross-cutting

Many cities/counties have a community indicators dashboard (like DataShare, Community Commons, or a local version). These aggregate data from multiple sources and are often the best starting point.
